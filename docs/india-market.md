## Sessions and venue constraints

| Applicable session | Continuous NSE cash trading (IST) | Data handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular pre-open | Continuous trading begins at **09:15**. The pre-open auction begins at 09:00. | Start a 15-minute opening range at 09:15. With five-minute bars labelled by start time, the 09:15, 09:20 and 09:25 bars complete that range at 09:30. |
| Before 3 August 2026 | Normally 09:15–15:30. | Honour date-specific changes and interruptions. |
| 3 August–6 December 2026 | F&O-eligible **cash equities** normally trade continuously 09:15–15:15. Other eligible cash equities normally trade continuously 09:15–15:30. | Use the effective-dated CAS eligibility flag from the security master and exclude closing auction bars. |
| From 7 December 2026 | NSE's published CAS 2.0 timetable restores the 15:30 continuous close for CAS-eligible cash equities. | Continue to exclude the subsequent cash auction. Derivatives hours are a separate schedule. |

The cash opening auction rules changed on 7 September 2026. The exchange accepts market and limit orders in the initial five minutes and shifts to limit-only entry with a randomized cutoff before matching and the 09:15 transition. Treat auction prints and their volumes as a distinct phase. Use timezone-aware Asia/Kolkata timestamps, preserve the original offset, and document whether feeds label bars at the start or end of the interval. [NSE opening auction](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-pre-open) · [NSE closing auction](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session) · [NSE CAS 2.0 FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-11/FAQs_for_CM_and_FO_FOR_CAS_2.0_20261109152618.pdf)

Maintain a capital-market **trading** calendar with dated overrides and consult exchange notices for outages or intraday halts. The 2026 annual circular is the baseline. A subsequent circular added the 15 January municipal election closure. Sunday 1 February was a live Union Budget session with standard continuous hours. The 8 November Muhurat session opened at 14:20, with continuous closes at 15:15 for CAS stocks and 15:30 for others. Mark exceptional schedules and news events when comparing sessions. Settlement-only holidays do not automatically close equity trading. [2026 capital-market holiday circular](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR71775.pdf) · [January election closure](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR72260.pdf) · [Union Budget session](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR72349.pdf) · [Muhurat session](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR76941.pdf) · [NSE and settlement holiday tables](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays)

Broker intraday cutoffs can precede the exchange close and change during volatility. On the verification date Zerodha listed 15:12 for cash CAS and 15:25 for other cash stocks. A simulation should use an early exit deadline and flag an unresolved position when a halt or a missing price leaves no usable fill. The exchange applies market-wide circuit breakers at 10%, 15% and 20% to the first qualifying move in Nifty 50 or SENSEX. A market-wide halt and stock-level price bands have distinct effects. [Zerodha intraday square-off bulletin](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/249809/latest-intraday-leverages-mis-bo-co) · [NSE circuit breakers](https://www.nseindia.com/products-services/equity-market-circuit-breakers) · [NSE price bands](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-price-bands)

## Universe and trading eligibility

- Begin with a dated Nifty 50, Nifty 100 or Nifty 500 constituent list or a manually defined watchlist, then join it to the day's NSE cash instrument master. Main-board `EQ` is a universe filter. Confirm equity instrument type, live status, settlement type, surveillance, price bands, tick size and intraday broker eligibility separately. Track `exchange`, `series`, `ISIN`, provider symbol/token, effective date and index/sector membership. Preserve old symbols and delisted instruments for historical work. The exchange and instrument identifiers distinguish an NSE listing from a BSE listing of the same issuer. [NSE securities available for trading](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/securities-available-for-trading) · [NSE daily reports and security master](https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports) · [Nifty 500 constituents and methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/broad-based-indices/nifty-500)
- Exclude trade-for-trade securities, suspended names, special auction or SME series and instruments outside the selected equity universe. Trade-for-trade requires individual settlement obligations and cannot be treated as an ordinary intraday net-off. Treat ASM, GSM, ESM and other restrictions as stage-dependent exchange facts. A strategy may conservatively exclude any flagged security. Read current surveillance files and broker intraday eligibility because neither an index membership nor an old clean snapshot establishes present eligibility. [NSE surveillance actions](https://www.nseindia.com/regulations/exchange-market-surveillance-actions) · [NSE GSM](https://www.nseindia.com/static/regulations/graded-surveillance-measure) · [NSE ESM](https://www.nseindia.com/static/regulations/enhanced-surveillance-measure-esm) · [Zerodha trade-for-trade explanation](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/general/articles/what-are-trade-to-trade-stocks)
- Read the actual tick size and lower/upper bands from the dated instrument file and quote. NSE uses price-linked tick sizes and reviews them periodically. For securities with derivatives, the continuous session uses a dynamic operating range in place of fixed daily stock bands. Scalps near a band can be one-sided. A candle touching a price does not establish executable depth or a fill. [NSE tick-size circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR67133.pdf) · [NSE daily price bands](https://www.nseindia.com/static/regulations/daily-price-bands-reports) · [Kite quotes and instrument fields](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/market-quotes/)
- Normal rolling cash settlement is T+1 on the applicable working-day calendar. Optional T+0 is a separate facility with its own eligible securities and trading hours. Long-only paper positions avoid the buyback and settlement complications of cash shorts. SEBI's short-selling framework permits retail participation, prohibits naked short selling and requires obligations to be honoured at settlement. Check current eligible securities, exchange disclosure requirements and broker restrictions before designing any short workflow. A locked upper circuit can prevent an intraday short buyback and leave a delivery obligation or auction exposure. [NSE cash market segments](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-segment) · [NSE T+0 cycle](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/t0-settlement-cycle) · [SEBI short-selling framework](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2024/framework-for-short-selling_80448.html)

## Market context and research parameters

These are research inputs. Preserve their values and timestamps as they were known at the signal, and validate thresholds out of sample. A feature without point-in-time coverage belongs in the review as missing.

| Input | Use in research |
| --- | --- |
| Nifty 50, sector indices and market breadth | Compare the stock's opening/session return with Nifty 50 and a mapped industry index, such as Nifty IT or Nifty Bank. Measure breadth over a dated, eligible universe and report the coverage. The line between broad market and sector strength is best measured on aligned completed bars. |
| India VIX | Record a timestamped implied-volatility level and change. NSE computes it from Nifty option quotes as an annualized expectation over approximately the next 30 calendar days. It supplies volatility context without implying price direction. |
| GIFT Nifty, INR and crude | Record the timestamp and contract month for the NSE IX GIFT Nifty futures quote and the reference used for the gap. It is a pre-open context input with its own basis and trading hours. Track USD/INR and crude where relevant to the issuer or market backdrop, using comparable timestamps. |
| Cash liquidity and execution | Review trailing median daily turnover in INR, spread in basis points, trade size as a share of observed volume, band headroom and quote freshness. Compare cumulative volume with earlier sessions at the **same elapsed trading time**. Use compatible regular sessions and omit exceptional sessions from baselines unless modelled explicitly. |
| Price structure | Use prior regular-session high, low and close, opening gap, opening-range width relative to ATR, session VWAP and distance to the stock's price bands. Align adjustment treatment on corporate-action dates. Preserve unadjusted execution prices and never mix adjusted closes with raw intraday bars for the same calculation. |
| Event risk | Check issuer results and board meetings, dividends and ex-dates, splits, rights, demergers, exchange halts, index changes, RBI MPC decisions, the Union Budget and MoSPI CPI/GDP releases. Mark whether each timestamp came from a scheduled release, a later revision or actual publication. Index derivatives expiry can be a context label even for cash trades. Use the current exchange contract calendar if tracking expiry. |
| FPI and DII flows | Use published aggregate cash flows as context. The exchange series is provisional, and a day's published number is available for research only from its release time. |

## Data sources, licensing and costs

- For reproducible local work, keep a provider-labelled copy of authorized input files outside version control and commit synthetic fixtures. NSE's capital-market UDiFF bhavcopy and security files provide end-of-day reference and point-in-time metadata. A daily file has no intraday ordering. Use authorized broker data or a licensed exchange feed for intraday bars, depth and tradeability checks. Budget for rate limits, access expiry, missing bars, corporate-action adjustments and differences between historical and live feeds. [NSE capital-market archives](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/historical-reports-capital-market-daily-monthly-archives) · [UDiFF formats](https://www.nseindia.com/static/resources/forms-formats-members) · [NSE paid data](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/real-time-data-subscription) · [NSE non-display data](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/non-display-data)
- NSE's official MCP provides a convenient path to permitted informational data, including bhavcopy-derived end-of-day history and breadth. NSE describes it as informational and educational and restricts real-time trading, commercial exploitation and AI/model training uses. Check the feed terms before storing, redistributing, publishing or sending data to a hosted AI service. SEBI's May 2026 circular sets a 30-day lag for certain sharing and use of market price data for educational purposes from 1 July 2026, with a specific exception for NISM's simulation lab. - In the example Zerodha NSE intraday schedule, brokerage is the lower of ₹20 and 0.03% per executed order, STT is 0.025% of sell value, combined NSE transaction and IPFT charges are 0.00307% of turnover, SEBI charges are ₹10 per crore, stamp duty is 0.003% on the buy side and GST is 18% on brokerage plus the applicable exchange and SEBI charges. ## Paper research and API execution

For historical replay, use session- and instrument-appropriate input cohorts. Build the universe and signals from data available before entry. Enter on the next admissible bar or an explicit fill event, never on an already-observed signal close. Test the opening gap through a stop, ambiguous stop/target bars, price-band or halt-related non-fills, missing data, and net P&L after charges. Separate the number of observed setups from the number of simulated paths drawn from them. More paths alone cannot repair a small or biased historical sample. SEBI's 2024 study reported losses for seven in ten individual intraday cash-equity traders in its study population. [SEBI cash intraday study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/jul-2024/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses_84948.html)

SEBI's retail algo framework and exchange implementation standards applied across brokers from 1 April 2026 under the published rollout. NSE treats client-direct API order messages as algo orders requiring appropriate tags, including messages at or below the 10-orders-per-second threshold. Lower throughput and human confirmation do not remove tagging obligations. The NSE 20 November 2026 clarification limits the self-hosted static-IP route to qualifying retail clients who develop their own algos using programming knowledge and requires a broker undertaking. Provider/broker-hosted arrangements and strategy registration have separate requirements. [SEBI framework](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html) · [SEBI rollout](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/sep-2025/extension-of-timeline-for-implementation-of-sebi-circular-dated-february-04-2025-on-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading-_96979.html) · [NSE client API FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQ_Retail%20Algo_03112025_NSE.pdf) · [NSE static-IP clarification](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INSP77559.pdf)

A future live adapter would require a broker-supported route with the applicable approvals, exact order review, durable order IDs, order/position reconciliation, partial-fill handling, cancel/replace rules and a broker-side stop or operator fallback. Use the broker's current product matrix and square-off schedule when assessing it. The repository's paper ledger and ticket review provide a way to rehearse the workflow with local data.
